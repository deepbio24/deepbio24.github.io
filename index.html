Projects

SeqA-mediated sister chromosome cohesion and DNA repair.

Spatial Control of DNA Repair by SeqA-mediated Sister Chromosomes Cohesion

The project asks whether SeqA-mediated sister chromatid cohesion modulates the efficiency and fidelity of homologous recombination-mediated double-strand break repair by influencing the spatial proximity of homologous sequences.

Experimental work combines I-SceI-induced site-specific breaks, CFU survival assays, mutation analysis, RT-PCR, and RecA-GFP time-lapse microscopy at snap and non-snap loci.

Full figures, hypothesis, experimental design, and key results are available on the Projects page.
